\title{TPMS Explorer: an interactive, gate-verified open ecosystem for triply periodic
minimal surface design, generative implicit fields, multifunctional physics simulation,
and biophysical tissue-ingrowth dynamics}

\author[a]{Anonymous Author}
\address[a]{Independent Open-Source Project}

\begin{abstract}
We present TPMS Explorer v7.0, a browser-based, zero-backend ecosystem for parametric design,
physics simulation, and additive-manufacturing preparation of triply periodic minimal surface
(TPMS) architected materials. The platform couples a watertight Surface Nets meshing pipeline
with thirty-six continuously executed CI gates ($>$1300 assertions) that verify every mathematical
claim shipped to the user. Version 7.0 adds five research-grade modules: (i) a sinusoidal-activation
implicit neural field (SIREN) with an exactly periodic Fourier-feature input and an 8-dimensional
latent space over five distilled TPMS experts; (ii) multiaxial anisotropic yield envelopes
(Hill-48, Tsai-Wu, Gurson, Drucker--Prager) with a unified star-convex ray-distance formulation
and an interactive 3D viewer; (iii) a Bloch--Floquet phononic band-structure solver built on
Born--von K\'arm\'an lattice dynamics with realified two-pass deflate-Lanczos eigensolution and
long-wave dynamical-matrix calibration; (iv) a reaction--diffusion tissue-ingrowth engine coupling
quasi-steady oxygen transport, hypoxia-gated osteoblast proliferation, and mineral deposition over
a 28-day horizon; and (v) a level-set topology-optimization loop with Hamilton--Jacobi Godunov
evolution driven by elastic and flow sensitivities under a volume constraint. All modules execute
client-side with zero dependencies beyond Three.js, export to industrial formats (STL, 3MF, GLB,
Abaqus INP, OpenFOAM polyMesh, G-code), and are guarded by behavior-level audit gates whose
calibration traps are documented.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method design}
\subsection{SIREN implicit field with exact periodicity}
A 4-layer sinusoidal network $F_\theta(\mathbf{x})=W_3\sin(W_2\sin(W_1\sin(W_0\,\mathbf{u})))
+\mathbf{b}_3$ consumes the Fourier feature $\mathbf{u}=[\sin x,\cos x,\sin y,\cos y,\sin z,\cos z]$.
Because every coordinate enters only through $\sin/\cos$, $F_\theta$ is \emph{exactly} $2\pi$-periodic
(measured deviation $2.2\times10^{-15}$), which makes cross-period water-tightness a property of the
architecture rather than of the fit. Five experts (Gyroid, Diamond, Lidinoid, Schwarz~P, fractal
trabecular) are distilled offline from the platform's single semantic source of TPMS fields with
Adam (RMSE $0.69\%$--$8.04\%$ at $16^3$, hardest for the multi-scale teacher). A latent code
$\mathbf{z}\in\mathbb{R}^8$ selects experts through
$m=\mathrm{softmax}(-\|\mathbf{z}-\mathbf{A}_i\|^2/2\sigma^2)$ with $\pm3$ Walsh anchor codes
(pairwise Hamming distance 4, cross weight $e^{-72}$). Lipschitz continuity is bounded by
$\prod_i\|W_i\|_2$ (power iteration) and cross-checked against sampled gradient suprema.

\subsection{Multiaxial yield envelopes}
All four criteria are expressed in a unified \emph{ray-distance} form: the distance $r(\hat{\mathbf{n}})$
from the origin to the envelope along a unit direction of principal-stress space. Hill-48 (with a
hydrostatic cap), capped Drucker--Prager, and Gurson close to star-convex bodies containing the
origin, hence $r$ is single-valued and the safety factor is exactly $r(\hat{\mathbf{n}})/\|\boldsymbol{\sigma}_0\|$.
Tsai-Wu admits a closed-form quadratic root. Gurson's hydrostatic pole
$(2\sigma_0/3)\,\mathrm{arccosh}((1+q^2)/2q)$ with $q$ equal to the porosity provides a strong
analytic anchor. Scaffold-level parameters are derived from the same Gibson--Ashby constants used
by the platform's energy-absorption module.

\subsection{Bloch--Floquet phononic bands}
The unit cell is discretized into voxel masses connected by 26-neighbourhood axial springs.
Bloch phases assemble a complex Hermitian $K(\mathbf{k})$, realified as
$\begin{psmallmatrix}A&-B\\B&A\end{psmallmatrix}$ so that all eigensolution machinery is real.
Two-pass \emph{deflate-Lanczos} (full reorthogonalization; the second run is deflated against the
first run's Ritz vectors) recovers both copies of every complex eigenvalue, preserving degeneracies.
Spring stiffness $\kappa$ is calibrated by exact inversion of the long-wave dynamical matrix
$\omega^2_{\mathrm{LA}}=\kappa h^2 k^2 S_x/(mN^3)$, $S_x=\sum \mathrm{d}x^4/\mathrm{d}^2$ --- affine
homogenization is explicitly \emph{not} the long-wave modulus of a central-force network (measured
ratio $0.53$, matching the analytic $0.54$). Under periodic boundary conditions rotations are not
admissible Bloch modes, so the $\Gamma$ point carries exactly three translational zero modes.

\subsection{Tissue ingrowth reaction--diffusion}
Oxygen obeys a quasi-steady diffusion--Michaelis--Menten balance solved by 7-point Jacobi iteration
with no-flux mirrors at solid interfaces and Dirichlet $C_0$ on the perfused boundary layer;
osteoblasts follow hypoxia-gated logistic growth with explicit daily updates; mineral accumulates
where oxygen exceeds a threshold. Operator splitting is justified by $\tau_{O_2}=L^2/D\approx0.05$
days versus a 28-day proliferation horizon. $V_{\max}$ is calibrated to a literature oxygen
penetration depth $L_{\mathrm{pen}}=\sqrt{2DC_0/V_{\max}}\approx0.4$\,mm. The gate verifies the
discrete mass balance (boundary-layer flux $=$ interior consumption, residual $0.000\%$) and
hypoxia gating on a dense-versus-porous pair.

\subsection{Level-set topology optimization}
The TPMS signed field evolves under $\partial\Phi/\partial t+V_n|\nabla\Phi|=0$ (Godunov upwinding)
with $V_n$ assembled from elastic von-Mises sensitivities (fully-stressed) and Darcy pressure-gradient
sensitivities, zero-meaned over \emph{interface} voxels, normalized, and constrained by a volume
Lagrange bias. Classical compliance under a unit service load is proportional to $1/\text{reaction}$
of the displacement-controlled solve --- the $\tfrac12 F\delta$ reading is a softening artefact under
displacement control. Islands are removed by largest-component BFS, $\Phi$ is reinitialized by an
exact 3D Euclidean distance transform, and the final field re-enters the platform's Surface Nets
pipeline through the precomputed-field injection contract, guaranteeing water-tight extraction.

\section{Quality control}
Thirty-six gates run via \texttt{npm run test:all} on three platforms. Every gate loads the shipped
TypeScript through a bundler and asserts behavior-level invariants (no mocked math). Gate
calibration itself is treated as a first-class activity: two documented false-red episodes
(a $\sqrt2$ scaling slip in the auditor's von-Mises conversion; a Gram--Schmidt normalization
threshold that silently skipped $10^{-10}$-scale columns) are recorded and their fixes covered by
regression assertions.
